==================================================
PHASE 21 — PRIVACY REVIEW
=========================
 
PROMPT:
 
Review the platform specifically for user privacy and PDPA compliance.
 
Focus on:
 
activity monitoring
presence
meeting media
notifications
supervisor visibility
retention of activity information
data subject access (users viewing their own history)
 
The inactivity system must be transparent.
 
Users should understand:
 
* what is being monitored
* what is not being monitored
* why the information is collected
* how long it is kept
* the legal basis for collection
Recommend data minimization.
 
Avoid surveillance patterns that are unnecessary for the product.
 
Verify the retention/expiry rules defined in Phase 2 are actually enforced (e.g. a scheduled job).
